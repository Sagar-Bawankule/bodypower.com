BodyPower has always showcased the very best talent from around the globe: world-class speakers on metabolic health, training, nutrition and human performance.

BodyPower Supertheatre speaker line-up. 2027 programme to be confirmed

  • Obesity · Appetite · GLP-1

    Prof. Giles Yeo

    Helping BodyPower audiences understand why body weight, appetite and obesity are far more complex than simply telling people to eat less.

  • Nutrition · Body composition

    Alan Aragon

    Evidence-led thinking around nutrition, muscle, fat loss and body composition in a world where weight-loss methods are changing rapidly.

  • Hormones · TRT · Longevity

    Dr Aamer Khan

    Exploring the growing conversation around hormones, testosterone, ageing and where medical treatment meets performance and lifestyle.

  • Metabolic health · Insulin

    Prof. Ben Bikman

    Exploring insulin resistance, metabolic health and the biology shaping some of the biggest changes in obesity, nutrition and health.

  • Prevention · Longevity

    Dr Michael Sagner MD

    Looking at how diagnostics, prevention and personalised health could reshape the relationship between fitness and medicine.

  • Nutrition · Coaching
