Where it began

A shared passion
for fitness.

Since 2008, BodyPower has brought together athletes, fitness enthusiasts, industry professionals and brands through a shared passion for fitness.

Today, we are building on that heritage through live events, clothing, training, nutrition and new fitness experiences.

BodyPower built its reputation around strength, physique and performance, and has always showcased exceptional talent from around the world.
